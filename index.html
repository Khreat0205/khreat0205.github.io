Kyeonghun Jeong

Machine Learning for Single-Cell Analysis and Clinical Omics

I develop machine learning methods for single-cell and clinical omics data. My work includes both algorithm development and collaborative translational studies.

Research Focus

Methods

CCI-GSVA · scMILD · scTELL · scDSTL

Sample-level cell-cell interaction scoring, weakly supervised subpopulation discovery, regulatory genomics, and adaptation of foundation models.

Translational Research

Kidney proteomics · Breast cancer genomics · Pan-cancer multi-omics

Clinical cohort studies using proteomic, genomic, and multi-omics data.

단일세포 및 임상 오믹스 데이터를 분석하는 머신러닝 방법을 개발합니다. 알고리즘 개발과 중개의학 공동연구를 함께 수행해 왔습니다.
